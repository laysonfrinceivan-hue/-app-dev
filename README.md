# One Piece
### Anime
* One Piece is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Eiichiro Oda. It follows the adventures of Monkey D. Luffy and his crew, the Straw Hats, as he searches for the legendary treasure known as the "One Piece" to become the next King of the Pirates. *
  
## E1 - I'm Luffy! The Man Who's Gonna Be King of the Pirates!
Countless souls have been lured along the Grand Line in pursuit of the legendary One Piece! Monkey D. Luffy is a young pirate with a dream: to prove the legend true and be king of them all!
## E2 - Enter the Great Swordsman! Pirate Hunter Roronoa Zoro!
Luffy and his new companion Coby set sail in search of the infamous pirate hunter Zoro, rumored to be held captive at the Marine Base.
## E3 - Morgan versus Luffy! Who's the Mysterious Pretty Girl?
Luffy is in the line of fire after saving Zoro, and now tyrannical Captain Morgan is out for their heads! Zoro must decide if he will join forces with Luffy or die in captivity.
